Design reference
----------------
The layout follows the academic-homepage template by Shitong Luo:
https://github.com/luost26/academic-homepage
Visual reference supplied by the owner: https://stevezs315.github.io/

This version implements the academic sidebar/card layout as dependency-free
static HTML/CSS/JavaScript. The reference template's license is retained below.
The owner's photographs, research figures, videos, and personal content are
not licensed by the template's license.
